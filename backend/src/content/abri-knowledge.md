# ABRI

## What ABRI is
ABRI is a trusted business network for Malaysia. Before you do business with a company, you should be able to see that it is real and that other businesses stand behind it.

- Every business can be found in the ABRI directory.
- A business that claims its listing and passes our check against the SSM register (Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia, the companies register) earns the **SSM-Verified** badge. No one can buy the badge.
- Verified members endorse the businesses they have actually worked with, so trust on ABRI comes from peers rather than advertising.

ABRI is not a digital business card app, not just a directory, and not a LinkedIn replacement. It is trust infrastructure: real, registered companies can prove they are legitimate, vouch for one another, and find counterparties they can deal with safely.

## Why ABRI exists (vision)
Open platforms are filling up with fakes: fake accounts, fake reviews, AI-generated businesses. It is getting harder to know who is real. ABRI's answer is two things no one can quietly fake: a check against the official SSM register, and endorsements from other verified businesses who put their own name on the line.

Our principles:
- Trust is the product. Paying unlocks features, never trust.
- Give first. ABRI makes it easy to endorse, thank and connect with businesses you've worked with, and status and leads flow back.
- No advertising, and we never sell your data or leads.

Over time ABRI aims to become the trusted, machine-readable record of Malaysian businesses that both people and AI purchasing tools can rely on.

## Where ABRI operates
We are starting in the Klang Valley (Kuala Lumpur and Selangor), then growing across Malaysia.

## The directory
- Anyone can browse and search the directory at /directory, without an account.
- Listings are built from public sources: open map data, government registers and businesses' own websites. The privacy notice lists them.
- That's why a business may be on ABRI without ever having signed up. An unclaimed listing is marked as seeded from public data.
- Visitors who aren't logged in see a business's name, area, address and website. Logged-in members also see phone numbers and email addresses.

## Claiming your business
1. Find your business in the directory, or add it if it isn't there.
2. Go to /register and claim it with your details and your business's SSM registration number.
3. If the listing already has an email address, we can send a 6-digit code there. Enter it and your claim is approved straight away.
4. Otherwise our team reviews your claim, usually within a couple of business days.

While your claim is pending you can log in and use the directory. Once approved you can edit your page, connect with and follow other businesses, and use your plan's features.

## Verification levels
- **Listed**: seeded from public data, not yet claimed.
- **Claimed**: the owner has claimed the listing.
- **SSM-Verified**: we have checked the business's SSM registration number against the SSM register by hand. This usually takes a couple of business days.

The SSM-Verified badge is the same on every plan, including Free. You cannot pay for it.

## Endorsements
An endorsement is a verified business saying publicly that it has worked with you and stands behind you. Endorsements carry the endorser's own name, so they can't be bought or quietly faked. You need to be SSM-Verified to give and receive endorsements. Endorsements can include a short testimonial.

## Network
Members can connect with other businesses, follow them, and see a trust feed of endorsements and verifications as they happen.

## NFC card
Plus and Pro members get one ABRI NFC card. Tapping it opens your verified ABRI profile, so the people you meet see that your business is real before they see your contact details.

## Plans and pricing
- **Free** (RM0, forever): listed in the directory, the SSM-Verified badge once checked, a full editable profile, the trust feed, your contact details visible to members, and a count of who viewed your profile.
- **Plus** (RM490 / year): everything in Free, plus giving endorsements (20 a month), accepting endorsements and testimonials on your profile, seeing which of a business's endorsers you already know, and one NFC card.
- **Pro** (RM1,490 / year): everything in Plus, plus 40 endorsements a month, seeing who viewed your profile, watching businesses for verification changes, and a record of who you checked.
- **Enterprise** (custom pricing, from RM3,500): for organisations. Contact us.

Joining is free. Paid plans are optional.

## Founding members
Businesses approved before launch become **Founding members**. They get the Pro plan free during the founding period and keep the Founding badge for good. After the founding period they move to the Free plan, which stays free.

## Changing or removing your details
Claim your listing. Once your claim is approved you can edit or remove the phone number, WhatsApp, email, website, address and other details from your profile.

## Personal data
If your personal data is on a listing you don't own, contact us through the Contact page (/contact) with a link to the listing and we'll look into it.
